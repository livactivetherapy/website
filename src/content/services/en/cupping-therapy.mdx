Cupping therapy is an alternative treatment method with roots dating back over 3,000 years. More commonly known as an Eastern medicine treatment, it has steadily increased in popularity over the years — especially after its visible use during the 2016 Summer Olympics.

## Dry Cupping

Dry cupping therapy involves creating a small area of low pressure next to the skin. A variety of tools can be used to accomplish this — the two most common being a mechanical pump and silicone cups.

Our therapists are trained in both the mechanical pump and silicone cup techniques. Following assessment, your therapist will decide which technique is most appropriate for your condition.
