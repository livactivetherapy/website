Our occupational therapy services are designed to assist in the recovery of optimal function after an acute or chronic condition or injury. Interventions are developed to meet individual needs and to address any dysfunctional compensations that were developed from the condition.

Patients are guided and educated on their condition, prognosis, and the prevention of future re-injuries. This educational approach is integrated into a comprehensive recovery program — so at the time of discharge, our patients leave feeling knowledgeable, stronger, and healthier long-term.
