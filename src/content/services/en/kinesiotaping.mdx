Therapeutic tape was originally developed during the 1970s by a Japanese chiropractor, Dr. Kenzo Kase, who sought to maintain patient progress between sessions. The method gained prominence in America during the 1990s — particularly through use by Olympic and professional athletes.

The tape itself is made of latex-free material with an acrylic adhesive that's heat-activated. Its fiber structure allows stretching up to 140% of its original length, enabling therapists to manipulate direction, shape, and location for specific therapeutic effects.

## How it works

The Kinesiotaping Method is a definitive rehabilitative taping technique that helps facilitate the body's natural healing process — providing support and stability to muscles and joints without restricting range of motion.

Kinesio Tape alleviates pain and facilitates lymphatic drainage by microscopically lifting the skin, creating skin convolutions that increase interstitial space and reduce inflammation in affected areas.
