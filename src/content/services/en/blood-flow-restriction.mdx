Blood Flow Restriction (BFR) training uses a wide-based tourniquet on a proximal limb to limit arterial inflow while blocking venous outflow — trying to mimic the hypoxic environment of high-intensity exercise.

## Why it works

BFR at low loads (20–30% 1RM) can significantly increase strength and hypertrophy in a similar way to lifting heavy (80% 1RM). It is also effective for increasing muscle protein synthesis (MPS).

## Benefits

- Improved strength and muscle growth
- Improved bone healing time
- Improved cardiovascular endurance
- Reduced muscle atrophy post-operatively
- Aids in muscle repair following damage
