Registration forms
===================

Drop your two registration PDFs here with these exact filenames:

  registration-english.pdf
  registration-spanish.pdf

Once they exist, the download cards on the Contact page and the
"Registration forms" section in the footer will work automatically.

Paths configured in src/config/site.ts:
  forms.en: /forms/registration-english.pdf
  forms.es: /forms/registration-spanish.pdf
